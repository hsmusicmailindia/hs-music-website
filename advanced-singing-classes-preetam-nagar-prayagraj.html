Frequently asked questions

Before your
first class.

Who can join Advanced Singing classes in Preetam Nagar?

The course is suitable for trained singers ready to improve control, range, expression and live performance. A trial class helps us understand the learner’s present level and goals.

How many classes are held each week?

Classes are generally conducted three days per week. Available days and batch timings can be confirmed with the branch team.

What is the monthly course fee?

The monthly course fee is ₹1,950. Registration or other applicable charges can be confirmed before enrolment.

Do students receive performance opportunities?

Yes. Suitable students may perform at HS MUSIC showcases, including BlueMoon, to build stage confidence and real audience experience.
