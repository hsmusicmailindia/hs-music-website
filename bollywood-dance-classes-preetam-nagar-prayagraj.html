Bollywood Dance at Preetam Nagar

Learn the moves.
Find your expression.

Our Preetam Nagar Bollywood dance classes help learners understand rhythm, body coordination and performance expression. Lessons move step by step from warm-ups and basic moves to complete choreography and confident presentation.

Students receive a lively, supportive environment with regular correction, fitness-building practice and opportunities to become comfortable performing in front of an audience.

Frequently asked questions

Before your
first class.

Can a complete beginner join Bollywood dance?

Yes. The course is beginner friendly and no previous formal dance training is required.

What do students learn in the first few classes?

Students begin with warm-ups, basic steps, rhythm counting and body coordination before moving to complete choreography.

How many classes are held each week?

Bollywood Dance is conducted three days per week.

Are stage opportunities available?

Yes. Suitable students receive opportunities to perform in HS MUSIC showcases and BlueMoon events.

Who teaches Bollywood Dance?

The Bollywood Dance programme is guided by Saumya Chandra with an energetic, performance-focused approach.
