Beginners Singing at Preetam Nagar

Build the foundation.
Find your expression.

Our Preetam Nagar vocal classes help beginners understand how to use the voice safely and musically. Lessons move step by step from breathing and pitch matching to rhythm, simple alankars, ear training and song presentation.

Students receive a friendly learning environment with regular practice guidance, correction and opportunities to become comfortable singing in front of others.

Frequently asked questions

Before your
first class.

Can a complete beginner join?

Yes. The course is designed for learners with no previous formal singing training.

What will students learn first?

Students begin with breathing, vocal warm-ups, pitch awareness, rhythm and simple alankars before moving to songs.

How many classes are held each week?

Beginners Singing is conducted three days per week.

Are stage opportunities available?

Yes. Suitable students receive opportunities to perform in HS MUSIC showcases and BlueMoon events.

Who teaches the course?

The Beginners Singing programme is guided by Deepanjali Mishra with a supportive, foundation-focused approach.
