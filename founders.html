THE PEOPLE BEHIND HS MUSIC

Two minds. One vision.
A stage for every artist.

HS MUSIC was built to give aspiring artists more than lessons—professional guidance, confidence, creative freedom and real opportunities to perform.

Meet the founders
Our beginning

Passion became purpose.
Purpose became HS MUSIC.

HS MUSIC began with a shared belief that music education should shape both skill and character. Harshit Srivastava and Kirti Gupta created an academy where students are trained carefully, encouraged personally and prepared to step confidently onto the stage.

Today, that belief lives through every class, performance, workshop, recording session and BlueMoon event organised by the academy.

01
Founder

Harshit Srivastava

A singer, mentor and creative leader focused on turning musical potential into confident performance.

Harshit Srivastava founded HS MUSIC with the vision of creating a professional yet welcoming space for artists in Prayagraj. His approach connects vocal technique, stage culture, microphone skills, studio awareness and personality development.

Advanced SingingPlayback SingingStage PerformanceStudio RecordingCreative Leadership

Co-Founder

Kirti Gupta

A vocal mentor and creative force dedicated to helping every learner grow with confidence and joy.

Kirti Gupta co-founded HS MUSIC and plays a central role in its student-first learning culture. Her mentoring combines vocal development, expression, personality building and supportive stage preparation, especially for young and developing singers.
